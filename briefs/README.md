# Briefs

One file per assignment: `<yyyy-mm-dd>-<scope>.md`, dated by when the brief was written.
A brief opens with its Telos (why the work is wanted, in terms that bear on the work), then: what to read first, what is decided, what to find out before the plan, the steps, the rules.
The planner plans first and shows the plan.
A new brief is the first commit on its `edit/` branch; it is not rewritten after the work begins. A correction is a new brief, or a note at its end.
